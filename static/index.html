HOW MUCH, AND WHEN

Fill now, or bridge to a better price?

Same driving · same ending fuel

Every 10 c/L drop saves $1 for each 10 L you postpone, before extra travel. Your fuel remaining and driving determine the smallest top-up; your tank size determines how many litres you can defer.

Example profile: 50 L tank, 10 L remaining, 35 km/day, 8 L/100 km. Adjust these before using the result.

Travel costs, discount & budget

Extra travel is the additional distance/time versus filling now. Zero assumes the later stop fits your normal travel. The same discount is assumed later.

OPEN NOTEBOOK

A forecast has to earn its place.

7-day horizon

We compare today’s price, a damped trend, a weekly pattern, and similar historical weeks. Models only see observations available before each forecast.

The method is selected on earlier dates, then measured on later dates with a seven-day gap between sets. The target is the median of each station’s last observed U91 price for that day.

Checking coverage…

Older price-change records without collection timestamps are excluded from training. Missing days remain missing.
